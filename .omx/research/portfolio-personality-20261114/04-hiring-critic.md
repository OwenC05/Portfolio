# Hiring critic: make the memorable thing carry evidence

Research date: 14 November 2026. Lens: a hiring reviewer assessing software engineering and applied-AI work. Research and discussion only; no direction is approved here.

**Recommendation:** retain the working-table idea, but make its contents identifiable evidence of Owen's work and interests. An ambitious artistic interface can strengthen the application if visitors can immediately understand his role, inspect a real engineering decision, and share a direct project link. A beautiful room cannot establish production experience.

## Three review depths

These are proposed design acceptance tests, not measured recruiter attention statistics.

| Review | What must become clear | Proposed route |
|---|---|---|
| 15 seconds | Owen Cheung; software/applied AI; concrete work; how to reach CV/contact | Visible role statement, compact work index, CV and contact links; no introductory interaction required |
| 90 seconds | One problem, Owen's contribution, one technical decision, evidence, current status | Open one project; see a short summary plus a real output or trace and its qualification |
| Deeper review | How it works, why choices were made, reproducibility, failures, ownership | Stable project URL → architecture/decision notes → specific code, run record, benchmark or demo |

Put Work, CV and Contact first in navigation priority, with GitHub directly reachable and About easy to find. A room's objects may duplicate these paths, but project discovery must not depend on hover, sound, exploration, or knowing what an object means. Project links should survive sharing and browser Back; readable content should remain available on mobile and when motion is reduced.

## Ranked evidence to show

This is a provisional editorial order based on the supplied content truth, not a fresh audit of the underlying repositories.

1. **Dissertation experiment: a compact, reproducible case.** Show the question, Qwen on the university GPU server, custom TF-IDF retrieval, a paired answer with its retrieved context, and a run/configuration reference. Keep “20 questions / 40 primary outputs / one GPU / fictional fixture” beside any results. Explain one limitation or failure. Do not convert counts into an effectiveness claim, imply a completed thesis, or imply continuously available inference.
2. **LNRS: professional context and contribution.** An employer-safe example of an instruction/script/skill/context workflow, with Owen's role and one documented adoption or delivery observation if available. It could move first for customer-facing roles when the evidence supports it. Using or configuring agent tools does not establish ownership of a custom production autonomous-agent backend.
3. **Application Copilot: inspectable execution.** Show a real, shareable recorded job, its stages, the evidence supporting a draft, and where human review occurs. Label development status. Separate existing synthetic workflow tests from the still-open end-to-end recovery and semantic-quality qualification. An animated workflow diagram is explanation, not test evidence.
4. **TypeForge: product behavior plus model boundary.** Pair a short genuine typing interaction with the Python benchmark, versioned model identity, and explanation of how the browser and model artifact relate. A decorative keypress is not this demo. Do not claim learning or typing-speed gains.
5. **Portfolio engineering: optional additional case.** If an ambitious interface is eventually built, publish actual design decisions, asset provenance, performance measurements, and accessible alternatives. This can demonstrate care and execution; it does not replace applied-AI evidence.

## Personality that helps recall

**Proposed:** give the table a few meaningful objects with clear captions, rather than four equally weighted hobby sections. Personal objects need not justify their existence through an engineering metaphor.

- **Custom keyboards:** strongest existing bridge to TypeForge. A real photograph or a specific build decision, once supplied, connects the person and product. Do not imply the generated key illustration depicts Owen's hardware.
- **Audio:** give listening/headphones/hi-fi and making music distinct room in the story. An owner-supplied listening note and a short original production excerpt with a process comment would communicate different interests. Any playback should be explicitly initiated and stoppable; the technical story remains understandable in silence. Equipment, genres and DAW are unknown.
- **Snowboarding:** an authentic photograph or short recollection could supply place, movement and a memorable human detail. Avoid invented resorts, achievements, or an obligatory “I iterate like I ride” analogy.
- **Coffee:** useful atmosphere or a genuine ritual if Owen wants to share it. Lowest priority as a differentiator until specifics exist; a generic cup says little about this person.

## The case for ambition, and its failure modes

**For:** a carefully authored spatial interface can show initiative, taste, interaction engineering, and the ability to make complex material inviting. The table could organize evidence naturally: an experiment notebook, a workflow trace, a typing product, and personal objects. The saved Jesse screenshot shows explicit signposts within the scene; spatial expression need not mean unexplained navigation. A recognizable composition could become a memorable way to refer back to Owen.

**Against:** a loading sequence, forced camera tour, tiny screen-within-screen text, or hidden project links spends the visitor's attention before establishing relevance. A polished generated showroom risks creating an authorship question if there is little inspectable work beneath it. More objects, shaders or animation will not by themselves make the portfolio more personal.

**Blind spot in this lens:** optimizing only for hurried screening can erase the quality the user actually wants. Do not reduce the idea to a conventional CV page with a coffee icon. Retain expressive ambition, then test whether it increases recall while preserving access to evidence.
