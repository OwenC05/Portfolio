# Studio apprentice robot: delivery plan

15 November 2026. Coordinator-owned. Orca run: `run_4261d20ca7d6`.

## Outcome and scope

Deliver a reference-inspired, interactive floating apprentice inside the 3D Studio. Its eyes, head and fins express curiosity and attentiveness; its ring presents the actual retrieved passages. Visitors can discover it, ask questions, inspect source excerpts, follow source/contact links and close the interaction without losing their place in the room. The same experience remains usable with keyboard, touch, reduced motion or unavailable WebGL.

Owen selected the apprentice personality and requested this detailed plan and delegation. The current execution wave develops the design and production/integration specifications. Subsequent implementation tasks are defined below and become dispatchable as their actual inputs are ready. Do not make speculative application changes while those contracts are being settled.

Confirmed constraints from the conversation: studio-only; actual Claude owns design; Codex owns engineering; Higgsfield/Blender concept development, Meshy asset production and Blender cleanup are the preferred sequence; keep assistant text generation off and reuse existing source-grounded behavior; preserve separately owned Studio work. No voice, permanent cross-site robot, retrieval expansion, model training, camera redesign or production publication is included.
