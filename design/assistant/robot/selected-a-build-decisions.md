## Geometry and appearance to retain

1. Head: slightly stretched ball, approximately 10% wider than tall, depth approximately equal to height; level neutral pose. These are visual proportions, not measured metre dimensions.
2. Seam: one thin brass ring over crown and under chin just behind the face; plain porcelain at the back, no horizontal equator seam.
3. Face: opaque curved dark panel slightly inset behind a thin brass rim; no transparent dome or cavity. Eye parts sit on this surface.
4. Eyes: warm emissive discs with dark pupils and one fixed highlight. Rest gaze centered horizontally and centered or very slightly high vertically. Do not model bloom spill.
5. Lids: retain the soft rounded lower lids, face-coloured, covering approximately the lowest fifth of each eye at rest. No upper lid or brow.
6. Bookmark: short brass post on the character's left crown, leaning slightly outward; rigid notched red ribbon hangs down its outer side. No cloth simulation or ambient sway.
7. Body: compact teardrop with a solid, slightly domed brass cap inside its collar; no dark top disc, hole or recess.
8. Finish: warm-white porcelain with soft even sheen. No baked window reflections, craze/crackle lines or photographic highlights. Prefer simple materials; generated textures must be inspected for these artifacts if used.
9. Tray: shallow round brass dish, approximately nine-tenths of the head width, level and empty at rest. Glow is material/presentation, not geometry or evidence that results exist. Existing truthful state rules govern any variation.
10. Separation: retain clear gaps between head, body, two mitten hands and tray. Keep expression-critical parts separately controllable for the agreed finite poses.
