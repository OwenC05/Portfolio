01 / Research engineering

A reproducible RAG experiment

 From a self-hosted model to a comparison that can be inspected and repeated.

What I built

A Qwen inference setup on a university GPU server, a custom TF-IDF retrieval pipeline, and a benchmark comparing plain-model answers with answers supplied with retrieved context.

What makes it inspectable

Twenty questions, forty primary outputs, recorded configurations and source provenance. The comparison looks at required facts, claim support, citations and abstention rather than treating fluent answers as sufficient evidence.

Where the evidence stops

This is an exploratory dissertation preparation experiment on a fictional teaching fixture. It is not a general performance claim, a completed thesis result, or a continuously operated model service.

02 / AI systems · In development

Application Copilot

 Making the evidence and the workflow around AI output visible.

The engineering focus

A local-first application workspace with evidence retrieval, inspectable citations, drafting and human review. Durable jobs and recorded stages make the workflow easier to inspect and recover.

Beyond a prompt

The work includes job state, idempotency, recovery behavior and controls around selected-data model calls. The aim is a workflow people can understand and supervise.

Current limits

Synthetic tests exercise the workflow behavior. Full end-to-end recovery and semantic quality qualification are the next milestones.

03 / Product + ML

TypeForge

 A browser product that connects a small interaction to an evaluated model.

The product

A local-first typing application built with native browser technology and local storage. The interface keeps the typing experience direct and the user's data close to the device.

The ML work

A Python benchmark compares a baseline and candidate model, with a versioned artifact used for browser inference. Product engineering and model evaluation are part of the same project.

What the benchmark means

Model evaluation is not evidence that users type faster or learn more. No such improvement is claimed here. The repository documents deployment and evaluation limitations.
