## Three dispositions needed for release

1. **Record approval or revision of the design-budget replacement, without implying it already happened.** The addendum calls itself a proposal but also says the old forecast is “replaced,” and its table/JSON call 20 calls and 25,000 triangles `contingencyHeldByRoot`. Root has not selected that reserve. Treat all replacement ceilings and reserves as author proposals until a recorded decision. Adoption must explicitly identify the superseded 70-main/130-peak planning limits and affected render-gate ledger criteria. Keep inventory/profile hashes and hard **200 calls / 200,000 rendered triangles / 1,500,000 combined compressed bytes** unchanged. The 140,000 proposed rendered-triangle design ceiling is an explicit change from 138,500, however small.

2. **Define one shared reserve and enforce row limits; stage slack is not extra free capacity.** Proposed stage ceilings sum to **115 main calls / 148 refresh calls / 86,500 placed triangles**. Those are compatible with the proposed 120-main and 165-refresh outer limits by themselves. But 148 plus a separately held 20-call reserve is **168**, exceeding 165. The consistent adoption is to treat stage overruns above the 141-call base as consuming the proposed 20-call reserve: reaching every stage refresh ceiling spends 7, leaving 13, so total remains 161. Alternatively root can choose different limits/reserve explicitly. This review chooses neither on root's behalf.

   If root prefers a separately additive reserve, **17 calls is the clean exact choice**: 148 stage ceilings + 17 reserve = 165. Relative to the 141 base this gives 7 stage-slack calls plus 17 root-reserve calls, totaling 24 design-headroom calls. This replaces the author's 20-call proposal; it is not an arithmetic reinterpretation of an already approved reserve. Root must name which model it selects.

   Apply the same consumption rule to the proposed 25,000-triangle reserve. Aggregate stage placed ceilings alone do not guarantee the 140,000 refreshed-triangle design ceiling: merely using M1's 13,882 placed-triangle slack as noncasting geometry raises the base to 117,266 refreshed triangles; adding a separate untouched 25,000 reserve gives 142,266. Every leaf split, triangle overrun, caster/sidedness change, added texture or extra pass requires an updated weighted ledger **before acceptance or dependent authoring**, even when a loose stage cap still fits. Use row ceilings as current commitments and stage caps as additional limits, not permission to override rows. The current validator does not enforce this addendum; root/technical review must compare each new manifest against it.

3. **Approve the required Claude-owned design assumptions and separate optional branches.** M3's base depends on same-asset baking, reduced material partitions/palette substitutions, keycap nocast behavior and single-sided `lib_frost`. T2's two palette cells are in the base, so approving the ledger must explicitly include that bounded library edit with existing cells preserved. The related palette/cloth substitutions, caster reductions and baked families in M4/M5 likewise need owner/root adoption as design choices. They are not merely count corrections. C1, C2, T1 and changes to retained M1/M2 remain separately scoped options until root releases them; their savings cannot be silently spent. D1/PC v04 follows root's separate conditional release and visual verdict, not this ledger review.

A recorded root disposition can resolve these points without rewriting or weakening the frozen inventory/profile. If the author files are revised later, preserve v01 and issue a new hash-bound version. Until disposition, this review is not execution authorization.

## Representation and validator feasibility

The proposed execution path fits the existing schema: one asset identity and material per primitive; same-asset disconnected parts baked in source; positive, finite transforms; allowed material names; no GPU instancing, runtime batcher, decoder or extra material extension. The validator explicitly allows boolean sidedness (`profile-core.mjs:207`), single primitives (`:217`), and BLEND leaves with nocast/no-receive flags and finite authored ordering (`:274`). It does not impose the inventory's old per-asset material lists or casting estimates as immutable export rules. Root adoption of the art changes is still required by the ownership/gate process.

Three representative paths are executable:

- **M3 keyboard and mouse:** bake all 67 caps into `kb_keycaps__caps` with palette UVs, retain a separate frost shell and opaque plate, and export the mouse under its own ID. The listed 9 main / 16 refresh calls follow without cross-asset merging. Frost must carry `transparent: true`, `occluder: false`, `nocast: true`, `receiveShadow: false`, depth-write disabled by the loader and an explicit finite numeric `renderOrder < 2`, with retained PC glass at 2 and contacts at 1. If Claude intends frost after contacts as well, use `1 < renderOrder < 2` (for example 1.5); the validator permits fractional finite values. Exact authored order belongs in the actual manifest. Opaque props remain blockers even when nocast.
- **M4 pads and controller:** four pad shells form one palette/casting leaf and four heads one weave/noncasting leaf; 29 white and 20 black keys form one palette/noncasting leaf. No “one shared mesh equals one draw” shortcut is used. Caster reductions and whether all required forms remain identifiable need Claude's visual review; the sub-texel shadow explanation alone is not appearance proof.
- **M5 panels and lights:** three panels become two leaves by material and five wall lights two leaves, preserving each asset ID. Added wall cable has its own row. Opaque/glow parts stay opaque blockers; textures must use supported embedded PNG/JPEG, UV0, dimensions/pixel limits and declared material slots. Any vertex colours remain RGB-only on the profile's permitted plaster/floor/weave materials, never a new palette-colour exemption.

The 107 leaves and 65,424 placed triangles fall below the unchanged 512/100,000 parser guards. That is feasibility evidence, not a synthetic final validator pass. Actual future bounds, normals, UVs, image decoding, sorting, blocker intersections, all 59 finished assets and absence of proxies remain export checks. The validator's final ID/proxy checks (`:340–341`) establish identity coverage, not detailed gear or visual completeness. Retained architecture is budgeted as-is; later subdivision/light-memory geometry cannot be smuggled in through M6 without a new ledger delta.
